How It Works 💡

Welcome to OrnaVision! This guide explains how to use our powerful AI search tools to find the perfect gold ornaments.

▣ Image Search

Upload a photo of an ornament you like, and our AI will analyze its features (design, shape, pattern) to find the most visually similar items in our database. You can click the Image Search tab or simply drag and drop a file into the upload area on the home screen.

◔ Text & Voice Search

If you don't have a picture, you can describe the ornament. For example, search for "Gold necklace with floral design".

Voice Search: Click the microphone icon next to the search box to speak your description. The system will automatically convert your voice to text and perform the search.

♡ My Collections

Found an ornament you love? Click the heart icon (♡) on any search result to save it to your collections. You can view all your saved items anytime by clicking My Collections in the sidebar. To remove an item, simply click the filled heart (♥) again.

↺ Search History

We automatically keep track of your previous searches so you never lose your train of thought. Check the Search History tab to view a log of your past Image, Text, and Voice searches, complete with a breakdown of your search habits.

⚖ Compare

Want to see the differences between two ornaments? Click Compare → on a search result, then select a second image to compare it against. Our AI will place them side-by-side and provide a detailed analysis of their similarities and differences.
